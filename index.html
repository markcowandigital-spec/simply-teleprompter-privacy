Privacy Policy

Simply Teleprompter · MARK COWAN DIGITAL LTD
Effective date: 18 October 2026

Simply Teleprompter is an Android app for reading scripts while recording videos. This policy explains what information the app accesses, where it is stored, and how you can manage it.

1. Information the app uses

The app requests access to your device's camera and microphone so you can record video and sound. It also processes scripts you type or paste, preferences such as text size and scrolling speed, and video recordings you create.

2. Where information is stored

Scripts, app preferences and retained recordings are stored locally on your Android device. You can choose to save a copy of a recording to a destination using Android's file picker. Once you save a copy outside the app, that copy is managed by the destination you selected, not by Simply Teleprompter.

3. Data collection and sharing

Simply Teleprompter does not require an account or connect to a developer-operated cloud service. The app is not designed to transmit your scripts, recordings or microphone/camera content to MARK COWAN DIGITAL LTD or to advertisers or analytics providers. Its core functionality runs on your device.

If you choose to save or share a recording using Android or another application, the privacy practices of the selected destination or service apply to that copy.

4. Permissions

Camera and microphone permissions are used to provide recording functionality. You can withdraw these permissions at any time in Android Settings. Recording will not work without the necessary permissions.

5. Retention and deletion

Scripts and settings remain on your device while the app retains its local data. Retained original recordings may be deleted using the app's recording-management controls. You can also clear the app's data or uninstall it to remove its locally stored information, subject to Android's normal storage behaviour. Copies you have saved to other locations must be deleted separately from those locations.

6. Security

The app uses Android's application storage and system file-selection mechanisms. We do not operate a server that receives or stores your scripts or recordings. As with other files stored on your phone, device security and any backups or third-party services you choose to use may affect the protection of exported copies.

7. Children

Simply Teleprompter is a general-purpose video-recording utility. It is not specifically designed for children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be published at this page, with the effective date updated where appropriate.

9. Contact

For privacy questions about Simply Teleprompter, contact MARK COWAN DIGITAL LTD at markcowandigital@gmail.com.
